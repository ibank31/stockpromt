## What the old agent did and did not record

The old handover did preserve a useful qualitative grouping: `klakon` examples leaned toward surreal landscape, urban fantasy, superhero animals, and typography-like graphics; other creator examples included isolated clipart, technical objects, food/produce, seasonal illustration, patterns, and backgrounds. It also preserved the important caveat that the white card is a notification presentation, not proof of source transparency or file extension.

What is missing is exactly what is needed for a factual high-demand conclusion: exact asset IDs, original file type, number of downloads per asset, impression-to-download rate, asset age, keyword/category data, account-level portfolio denominator, duplicate exposure, and independent buyer feedback. The old record therefore supports hypothesis generation, not demand ranking.

## Decision for StockForge

The recovered evidence changes the research priority from “abstract B2B niche first” to **recognizable commercial asset with a concrete buyer job**. It does not justify copying the original objects or claiming one of the screenshot niches is high-demand.

The lowest-burden candidate for a first JPEG trial remains **Technical Mechanical Component Illustration**, because the motif recurs twice, is isolated, has a concrete technical buyer job, and avoids people/release burden. It must be researched as a demand hypothesis, not treated as fact. The simplest low-cost candidates after it are `simple symbolic object illustration` and `food/produce illustration`, but their broad keywords are likely competitive and their value depends heavily on exact utility/context.

Character scenes, seasonal/year-specific artwork, and text-based quote graphics should not be first trials due to IP, expiry, text-rendering, originality, or metadata burden. Nature/food macro photography is a separate JPEG photo route and must not be simulated by illustration prompts.

## Next safe research

Before any generation, create a shortlist backed by three distinct evidence layers: official marketplace guidance for buyer use and file/category rules; public catalog supply/competition signals; and a controlled internal trial with human visual/commercial review. None of the recovered screenshots alone can supply the third layer. No GPU call, Kaggle run, upload, or submission was performed during this recovery.

## Validasi metodologi demand dari Adobe

Adobe menyarankan contributor memilih niche lalu mempelajari kebutuhan calon buyer, melakukan riset supply dan demand di niche spesifik, memantau berita/iklan/inspiration galleries, dan menggabungkan evergreen dengan seasonal/trending content. Adobe juga menyebut blank search, curated collections, dan Contributor Insights sebagai sumber sinyal aktual; panduan tersebut tidak menyatakan bahwa satu screenshot earnings cukup untuk memvalidasi demand. [1]

Implikasi: recovery 31 records di atas harus dipakai untuk menemukan pola berulang dan merumuskan hipotesis, bukan untuk menyimpulkan “niche demand tinggi”. Untuk target pecah telur, kandidat harus melewati tiga lapisan: (a) official buyer/content-need signal, (b) supply/competition dan utility check, dan (c) satu trial internal dengan human review dan ledger. Tidak ada lapisan (c) dari screenshot lama.

## Adobe Call for Content yang lebih spesifik

Adobe Artist Hub menyatakan koleksi Vectors and Illustrations berada pada demand tinggi untuk membantu creative professionals bekerja lebih cepat. Arahan yang relevan dengan evidence lama mencakup: single icons dan icon sheets; creative design elements seperti high-resolution/editable backgrounds, textures, seamless industrial textures, nature-based textures, fabric/craft elements, confetti/bokeh overlays; simple phrases/numbers and words; science and technology assets; modern business and retail; medicine/wellbeing; serta colorful poppy cartoons. Adobe memberi contoh bahwa regionally specific food, homes, activities, clothing, dan diverse illustrations dapat memperluas commercial appeal. [3]

Ini adalah evidence **content-need guidance** yang lebih kuat daripada screenshot earnings, tetapi masih bukan angka download per niche. Untuk first-sale low-cost, sinyal paling dekat dengan produk screenshot adalah `individual objects that instantly and clearly communicate a single idea`, creative components, recognizable food/nature, dan selected technical/science assets. Single icons/icon sheets bukan jalur JPEG utama; Adobe menyebut single icons sebaiknya SVG dan icon sheets AI/SVG, sehingga tidak boleh dipaksa masuk ke JPEG.

## Kontrak scoring internal yang harus dipakai

Registry `MarketOpportunity` StockForge mewajibkan `MarketEvidence` bertimestamp, buyer profile, result count bila tersedia, demand/growth/saturation/buyer fit/visual differentiation/variation/commercial clarity scores, dan risk flags. `opportunity_score` adalah heuristic internal, bukan marketplace sales probability; jika risk flags ada, recommendation tetap `REVIEW`. Recovery ini tidak menambahkan score baru karena evidence screenshot lama tidak memiliki denominator dan lane linkage yang cukup.

Aturan kerja untuk shortlist first-sale: **demand_score tidak boleh disebut fakta tinggi** hanya dari screenshot atau result count; result count dipakai sebagai supply signal, dan screenshot earnings dipakai sebagai anecdotal contributor evidence. Kandidat hanya dapat naik setelah ada official buyer/content-need signal, supply/competition observation, concrete buyer profile, low-cost production path, dan satu controlled trial yang direview manusia.

## Selected niche identity

**Lane:** `sewing_craft_tool_clipart`  
**Brief:** `sewing_craft_tool_clipart--beginner-kit`  
**Asset:** one square JPEG illustration containing a compact controlled cluster of five to six unbranded sewing/textile-craft tools: fabric scissors, colorful thread spool, soft measuring tape, thimble, pincushion, and seam-ripper-like tool. The cluster must remain one coherent mini-set with no human, no face, and no text.  
**Style:** cheerful hand-drawn clip-art / polished cartoon illustration, bold consistent dark outline, flat bright fills, restrained paper-grain texture, small offset shadow, compact silhouette, warm-white background.  
**Palette:** cobalt blue, coral pink, sunny yellow, teal, warm cream, and charcoal outline.  
**Buyer jobs:** beginner sewing lesson, sewing pattern tutorial, craft blog article, textile workshop handout, handmade-business social graphic, and unbranded craft-store packaging.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not include Adobe UI, the Adobe logo, email layout, buttons, dollar amounts, interface text, watermark, brand mark, copyrighted character, celebrity likeness, realistic hand, human face, generic construction tools, wrench, drill, gear, spark plug, motorcycle engine, tool box, busy workshop scene, readable labels, pseudo-typography, or a random pile of unrelated objects. Do not give tools cartoon faces; keep the style friendly through shape and color rather than protected character expression. Avoid unsafe sharp-point emphasis and avoid claims such as “safe for children” or “professional-grade.”

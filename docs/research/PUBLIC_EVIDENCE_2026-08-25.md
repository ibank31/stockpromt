## Adobe Stock — Create what's in demand
URL: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/artist-hub-migration/creat-what-s-in-demand.html

- Adobe advises contributors to choose a niche, study potential buyers, monitor news/advertising, browse curated collections, and use contributor insights.
- Adobe recommends mixing evergreen with seasonal/trending content because trend demand can be time-limited.
- Adobe best practices emphasize commercially appealing imagery, thinking about how buyers will use content in banners/print/TV, realism, diverse representation, genuine moments/places, careful wardrobe/props/technology choices, and avoiding risk/controversy.
- Interpretation: official platform guidance supports buyer-job clarity and commercial utility, but it is guidance, not evidence of sales for any specific micro-niche.

URL: https://www.gettyimages.com/visualgps/creative-trends/technology/2026-visual-trends-were-tracking

## Public horticulture evidence for seed-starting buyer job

### University of Minnesota Extension
URL: https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/starting-seeds-indoors

- The extension guide describes indoor seed starting as a relatively inexpensive gardening project and discusses planning, buying seed, location, containers/trays, mixtures, and timing.
- The page includes visual references to seedlings/transplants in containers on a tray and instructs users to identify containers or trays with labels.
- Interpretation: this supports a concrete educational/tutorial buyer job for recognizable seed-starting visuals, but it is horticultural practice evidence rather than stock-sales evidence.

### Royal Horticultural Society (RHS)
URL: https://www.rhs.org.uk/propagation/how-to-sow-seeds-indoors

- RHS defines and repeatedly uses seed trays/modules/pots in an eight-step indoor sowing workflow: fill tray, firm compost, sow, cover, water, label, cover/propagate, and place in a warm sunny spot.
- RHS notes indoor sowing is useful for tender and half-hardy ornamentals and vegetables, and for getting plants off to an early start.
- Interpretation: the tray is a globally intelligible object with a clear process context; the same source also makes seasonality and plant-specific accuracy relevant, so metadata should avoid unsupported species or growing claims.

## Adobe compliance and metadata evidence

### Generative AI content guidelines
URL: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/generative-ai-content-guidelines.html

- Adobe requires contributors to have the necessary rights to submit generative-AI content commercially and to review the tool terms.
- Adobe prohibits names of artists, real people, fictional characters, copyrighted works, government agencies, third-party IP, and descriptions implying actual news events in generative prompts, titles, or keywords.
- Adobe requires the Created using generative AI tools label for content created with generative AI software. A fictional-people/property checkbox applies when recognizable fictional people or property are present; no release is required when there are no recognizable people or property.
- Adobe distinguishes Photos (camera/photo-realistic subjects) from Illustrations (artistic illustrations or fantasy concepts). The StockForge route should therefore use `Illustrations` only if the result visibly reads as an illustration; otherwise the route must be reconsidered during review.
- Adobe quality guidance says to submit only content with unique value and not to submit multiple versions from the same or similar prompts.

### Titles and keywords
URL: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/content-policies-guidelines/metadata/tips-effective-titles-keywords.html

- Adobe recommends titles ideally under 70 characters, focused on the most visually important content, avoiding overly technical or gear-heavy terms and IP/real-person references.
- Adobe permits up to 49 keywords per submission; keyword order matters, with the first 10 having the greatest influence on search ranking.
- Keywords should be relevant nouns/adjectives/concepts specific to the visual and should reflect how a buyer would search.

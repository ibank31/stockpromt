## Evidence received

The attached evaluation assigns the current eight-icon file-flow product a final score of **8.0/10**. Its component scores are visual quality 8.2, consistency 8.8, UI usability 8.5, buyer appeal 7.7, uniqueness 6.8, commercial value 7.5, and stock potential 7.9. It estimates purchase interest at 25–35% for a buyer specifically seeking a file/cloud-management icon and 5–12% for a general thumbnail browser. The evaluation explicitly labels those percentages as product-appeal estimates rather than measured marketplace conversion rates.

The evaluator identifies the strongest assets as visual identity, cross-icon consistency, recognizability, small-size legibility, digital-product suitability, and the ability to extend the visual DNA into a larger system. The main weaknesses are medium uniqueness, generic subject matter, only eight icons, limited style/color variants, and optical weight imbalance in upload/download arrows.

The central product recommendation is to keep the current visual DNA and expand it into a professionally structured Cloud & File Management Icon System, potentially with 50–100+ icons, multiple style/color variants, and additional deliverables such as SVG, PNG, Figma, AI, and EPS where each format can be independently produced and validated. The evaluator distinguishes the current product as “nice to have” and a complete system as a more useful professional asset.

## Conservative interpretation for StockForge

This is valuable external expert evidence for product direction, but it is not a sales dataset, a buyer survey, a platform acceptance decision, or proof that the suggested price ranges will be realized. The 8.0/10 score may be recorded as an expert-review observation only after the user confirms that the attachment is intended to evaluate this exact trial. It must not be converted into synthetic category scores in the existing evaluation ledger without explicit user instruction.

The feedback supports a product-development hypothesis: **preserve the established teal/orange identity and consistency, then increase value through semantic coverage, optical refinement, distinctiveness, and packaging**. It does not authorize generating 50–100 icons, adding PNG/AI/EPS/Figma routes, or performing any marketplace upload. Those would require separate research, technical gates, budget/quota decisions, and explicit future authorization.

Adobe or other marketplace acceptance remains unproven. The current trial’s local structural PASS is separate from portal validation, legal declarations, GenAI disclosure, similarity review, and actual sales outcomes.
